ShrimPosture: External data
This folder contains the 3D models and mechanical design files for ShrimPosture, our interactive shrimp desk companion.

Design Overview
Our design is inspired by a shrimp sitting in a shell-shaped chair. The goal is to create a small character that can physically bend forward to remind users when they move too close to their computer.
We are designing the project in separate parts so we can access the electronics, adjust the mechanism, and make changes without rebuilding the entire model.

Main Parts

1.Shrimp Body
The shrimp is designed with a curved body and a hollow interior to accommodate its movement mechanism.
2.Shell-Shaped Chair
The chair supports the shrimp and connects it to the electronics enclosure.
3.Electronics Enclosure
A square base designed to house the microcontroller and electrical connections.

The enclosure has a removable bottom lid for access to its internal components.

4. Movement Mechanism
We plan to use a servo motor to make the shrimp bend forward and return to its original position.

The articulation system still needs to be developed and tested.
